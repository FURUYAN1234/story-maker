# Story Maker v5.5.1

## English
### What's New
- [stop] Keep one red emergency stop at the upper right during generation, including long-form work. Duplicate inline controls are hidden while their existing internal stop handlers remain connected.
- [guides] Updated the operation/API guides and version labels.
