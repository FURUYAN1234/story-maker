# Story Maker v5.3.8

## English

### What's New

- [sol61] Added GPT-6.1 Sol to the thought-model selector and made it the default in development and public builds.
- [fallback] Preserved Astra as a selectable option and existing lower-model fallbacks. Selecting Sol 6.1 does not automatically escalate to Astra.
- [pricing] Updated the displayed Standard short-context price date and documented the common generation, review and brush-up route.

## 日本語

### 更新内容

- [fallback] Astraの手動選択と既存下位モデルへのフォールバックを維持します。Sol 6.1選択時にAstraへ自動昇格しません。
- [pricing] Standard短文料金の表示日付と、物語生成・講評・ブラッシュアップの共通経路説明を更新しました。
