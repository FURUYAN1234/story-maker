# Story Maker v5.4.5

## English

### What's New

- [models] Added GPT-6.1 Terra to OpenAI story generation, editorial review, brush-up and image analysis. Sol 6.1 remains the default; fallback from Sol 6.1 can try Terra, while selecting Terra does not escalate to Sol or Astra.
- [pricing] Updated the Standard short-context model price display as of October 27, 2026. Terra is USD 1 input and USD 5 output per million tokens.
- [nano-contract] Synchronized the chibi drawing recipe with Nano's current clothing continuity rule. The release gate compares Nano's emitted contract rather than a sample script.
